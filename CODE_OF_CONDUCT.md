# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Assuming good faith, and asking before concluding
- Giving and accepting review feedback on the work rather than the person
- Being explicit about uncertainty, especially in an area where a mistake acts on
  someone's phone
- Making space for people who are new to the codebase

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/leestana01/autobile/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.
